## Photos and videos

The food, catering and wedding photos and video clips are stock media from
[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/), used under the
[Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free for commercial and
personal use, no attribution required, modification allowed.

The Pexels licence does not allow:

- selling unaltered copies of the photos/videos (e.g. as prints or on stock sites)
- implying that identifiable people shown endorse the business
- showing identifiable people in an offensive or misleading way

For that reason the site describes these as representative images, never as
photographs of our own events, staff or customers.

- Full list with links to each creator: `credits.html` (regenerated by
  `python tools/stock_media.py apply`)
- Source list: `data/media-library.json`
- Visual review sheet: `tools/media-review.html` (run `python tools/stock_media.py review`)
