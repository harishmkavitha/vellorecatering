MEDIA STYLE SOURCE OF TRUTH
===========================

Applies to all future business-critical page batches.

1. All people shown must look Indian / South Asian and contextually suitable for Vellore-area catering pages.
2. Do not reuse near-identical faces, outfits, poses, lighting or compositions within the same page.
3. Across different pages, vary:
   - face shapes and ages
   - costumes / outfit colours
   - camera angle and distance
   - lighting situation (day / sunset / evening / indoor)
   - scene type (ceremony, food, lounge, kitchen, counter, buffet, dessert, beverage, family dining)
4. Responsive hero variants may be alternate crops of the same hero source, but all other page visuals should feel distinct.
5. No competitor names, logos, signs, watermarks or branded backdrops.
6. Avoid blur, low-detail faces, repeated cloned guests and repetitive scene structure.
7. Every page should visually support that page's specific content and service logic.
