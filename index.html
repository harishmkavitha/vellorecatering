Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you cater in?

We are based in Kosapet, Vellore, and cater across Vellore city, Katpadi, Sathuvachari, Ranipet, Arcot, Gudiyatham, Ambur and nearby towns. Call us with your venue to confirm.

Do you offer both vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus?

Yes. We cook traditional vegetarian menus, including menus without onion and garlic, and non-vegetarian menus with seeraga samba biryani, Chettinad chicken and more.

How early should we book?

For weddings in the busy muhurtham months such as Thai, Vaikasi, Aavani and Karthigai, book two to three months ahead. For smaller functions, a couple of weeks is usually enough, but earlier is always safer.

How is the catering price worked out?

Pricing is per plate and depends on the menu, number of dishes, guest count, service style and venue. Send your date and guest count on WhatsApp and we will share a quote.

Do you provide banana-leaf service and buffets?

Yes, both, along with live counters for dosa, appam and chaat at receptions.
